# Prompt: turn OVERVIEW.md into a branded client document for Eros Cafe

Copy everything below the line into Claude, attaching `OVERVIEW.md` and the screenshots in `docs/screens/`.

---

You are producing a polished, branded document for the owner of **Eros Cafe** (a dessert café and coffee house with cafés in Manchester, Wigan, Urmston and St Helens) from **Integrate**, a web design and growth studio run by Jack. The attached `OVERVIEW.md` is the single source of truth: every finding, number, score and claim in your document must come from it. The attached screenshots are the only images you may use (file names tell you what each one is).

## Purpose
Two things at once: give the owner a genuinely useful free audit of their current website and online presence (useful even if they never hire us), and make choosing Integrate for the full build feel like the obvious next step. The document should read like a premium studio's audit and proposal: generous, specific, calm and confident.

## Format
A4 portrait PDF (or a document that exports cleanly to PDF), 12 to 18 pages, with a cover, numbered sections, generous margins and large photography. Use real page furniture: running header with "Eros Cafe · Homepage concept and growth review", page numbers, and a small "Prepared by Integrate" on each page.

## Brand system (match the new website exactly)
The document is dark and gold, like the new homepage: black marble, gold and cream, with heavy serif headlines.

Palette, with roles:
- Ink `#0B0908`: page background for covers, section openers and photo spreads
- Cocoa `#16100D`: alternate dark pages and panels
- Marble `#1E1814`: raised panels, tables and callout boxes on dark pages
- Wine `#1D0E10`: one deep accent spread (use once, for "Your new homepage")
- Gold `#D4AE64`: the brand accent. Headline highlights, numerals, rules, star ratings, the diamond mark and primary buttons (ink text on gold)
- Gold light `#EAD3A0`: hover-style highlights and small details on gold
- Cream `#F5ECDF`: primary text on dark, and the background for long reading pages (use ink text on cream for the audit tables so they print well)
- Oat `#B8AB9C`: secondary text on dark
- Ash `#8E847B`: captions and footnotes

Typefaces: headings in **Fraunces** (semibold 600, tight leading of about 0.95, sentence case, slight negative tracking; set big, with one phrase in gold where it earns it, as the homepage does with "from 8am."); body and labels in **Instrument Sans** (regular 400 for body, semibold 600 for small labels). If they are unavailable, use Georgia or Iowan Old Style for headings and Helvetica Neue for body.

Logo: `public/img/logo.png` (gold crest with "EROS CAFE"; always on ink or cocoa, never on a busy photograph). Integrate's own mark should appear small on the cover and in the footer only.

Layout rules: a 12-column grid; square corners everywhere; hairline rules (1px, cream at 10% on dark, ink at 12% on cream) rather than boxes; the gold diamond (a small rotated square) as the bullet and section marker, as on the website; section labels in the style "01 · Your current site"; photographs full-bleed or on the grid, never with drop shadows or rounded corners; no icon packs; no stock gradients. Tables for comparisons (competitors, Lighthouse scores) set in Instrument Sans with hairlines and gold numerals.

Tone of voice: confident, warm, plain English written for a busy business owner. Short sentences. No jargon (explain "schema" and "Google Business Profile" in a few words the first time). UK English. Second person ("your café", "your guests").

## Structure (use these sections, in this order)
1. **Cover**: title "A new homepage for Eros Cafe, and a plan to be found", the date, "Prepared for Eros Cafe by Integrate", one new-homepage screenshot.
2. **A short personal opening** (half a page): why we looked at Eros (a strong brand, a weak website), what is in this document, and that the homepage concept is already live for them to look at on their phone.
3. **Your free site audit**: "What's working" and "What's holding you back", each point backed by a specific example or screenshot from OVERVIEW.md, including the Lighthouse scores for the current site and what they mean in plain terms.
4. **How customers find you now, and who they find instead**: the search and Google Maps findings, the competitor table, and the Google Business Profile findings for each café.
5. **What a weak online presence is costing you**: only the estimates in OVERVIEW.md, each clearly labelled "estimate" with its reasoning; no invented numbers.
6. **Quick wins you can do this week**: the list from OVERVIEW.md, written as instructions the owner can follow themselves.
7. **Your new homepage, before and after**: the concept and why it fits Eros, the scroll journey section by section with screenshots, the signature moment (their own cappuccino, steaming), and how it converts (book a table, order to collect).
8. **How we fix the rest**: the full website build with hosting and ongoing edits; SEO automations (content and blog engine for the searches they are missing, a page for every café and every service, schema and technical fixes, automated Google Business Profile posts, rank tracking and a monthly report); review automation (NFC and QR review plates plus automatic review requests after each visit); AI lead capture (instant replies to every enquiry, a website assistant and a WhatsApp agent that answers questions, takes bookings and books catering, event and franchise enquiries into the calendar). Tie each one to a specific problem found in sections 3 to 5.
9. **What changes in the first 90 days**: a simple month-by-month view of what goes live and what the owner will see (more bookings by phone and online, more reviews, visibility for the missing searches).
10. **Next step: book a call with Jack**: one page, one action, Jack's contact details exactly as given in OVERVIEW.md, and the live homepage link.

## Rules
- Every claim must be traceable to OVERVIEW.md; if something is not there, leave it out.
- Label every estimate as an estimate and keep its reasoning.
- No prices, packages or fees anywhere: pricing is only discussed on Jack's call.
- No guarantees of rankings, traffic or revenue.
- Do not invent reviews, awards, statistics or quotes. Use only the Google reviews quoted in OVERVIEW.md, attributed exactly as there.
- Name the generated steam on the hero cup as a placeholder to be replaced with their own footage (OVERVIEW.md explains it).
- Keep it generous: the audit sections should be useful on their own.
